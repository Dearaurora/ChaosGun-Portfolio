# Map Spec

**Map Name**
- `Commercial Slice A`

**Version Goal**
- Build a `Boomerang Fu`-inspired medium-connectivity whitebox arena that is larger than the current map and centered on ring-out combat.

**Target Experience**
- Players have enough room to rotate, reset, and re-enter fights instead of staying in one tiny brawl pocket.
- Every main route reconnects into the rest of the arena so pressure can be answered with repositioning.
- Islands are separated by real lethal gaps, making bridge fights and ledge pressure meaningful.
- The center remains important, but side routes and neighboring islands create frequent flank and counter-flank moments.

**Planar Layout**
- One central main island labeled `C`.
- Four side islands labeled `N`, `S`, `W`, and `E`.
- Four main bridges labeled `CN`, `CS`, `CW`, and `CE`.
- Four secondary links labeled `NW`, `NE`, `SW`, and `SE` connecting neighboring side islands.

**Size Rules**
- Main island: roughly `28 x 28` units.
- Side islands: roughly `14 x 14` units.
- Main bridges: roughly `5` units wide and `6-8` units long.
- Secondary bridges: roughly `3.5-4` units wide and `6-8` units long.
- Gap width: roughly `6-8` units of clear lethal space between islands.

**Combat Rules**
- Spawn zones sit behind side islands rather than on the center island.
- Every spawn area must offer one faster return-to-center route and one safer reposition route.
- The center should stay mostly open, with only light interruptions that support short pauses rather than bunker play.
- Bridge mouths should connect into cut edges or angled entries rather than flat head-on choke points.

**Forbidden Patterns**
- No fake cliff edges that look lethal but are secretly safe.
- No large props or columns blocking readability at bridge mouths.
- No fully enclosed safe rooms on side islands.
- No spawn points placed directly on exposed cliff lips.

**Pass Criteria**
- All islands and routes are mutually connected with no forced dead-end pathing.
- Ring-out opportunities are consistently present around bridges, cut edges, and outer island rims.
- Players can recognize center, side islands, bridge hierarchy, and danger zones from the default camera.
- The whitebox already feels good to move and fight in before any heavy art pass begins.
